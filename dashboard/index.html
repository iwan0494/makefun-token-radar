reading the snapshot…

Every protocol still waiting on a token, priced by the record it already has.

Nobody knows a token's price before it trades. What the snapshot does hold is the market's own answer for protocols like each one: deep TVL, a record of how many days it has run without a launch, and a category that traders price differently. This radar is that answer, on one line, at one horizon, for all 198 early protocols — and a plan for what to do with each before the launch comes.

One line, five terms

Fitted from the 81 traded tokens in the snapshot that have a market cap and a year behind them. No protocol name is special-cased: every term below comes from the numbers DefiLlama records.

log cover = a + b log TVL + tilt + growth + record

implied value = TVL × exp(log cover) at one year past the listing day

Cover is market cap per dollar of TVL. The band is ±0.9 in logs: the range one record's residual spans.

Is it right before it is read?

The fit is tested only on what the snapshot can test: a record priced by a model that never saw it, a listing priced the same way, and rows re-derived in this page to check they match the file.

Every protocol in the snapshot, accounted for

The board is a choice: what is ranked, what is not an early protocol at all, and what the snapshot records as already trading.

What this board does not claim. It does not predict whether or when any protocol issues a token. It prices a record as it stands, at one stated horizon, by the market's own answer for protocols of the same size, family and age; the implied value is this entry's claim, never make.fun's. A protocol that records no token may still have one its DefiLlama record does not carry. Nothing here is investment advice, and the plans are built on the assumptions stated above, not on promises.
